===== DESCRIPTION (paste as is) =====
BTB Finance is the liquidity provider app that pays its users. Find pools with real volume across Ethereum, Base, BNB Chain, Robinhood Chain and Arc, simulate any range before you deposit (fees, impermanent loss, 30 day backtest), then add, compound, rebalance and remove positions on Uniswap V3 and V4, PancakeSwap, SushiSwap, Aerodrome, Giga, Ramses and UP from one screen. Everything is free. BTB earns a fee on swaps routed through the app and shares that revenue with users every Friday in BTB.

BTB is the asset of the ecosystem. Holding 10,000 BTB unlocks LP range alerts; 10,000,000 BTB unlocks the full BTB Agent, an assistant that reads your portfolio and finds your next pool. OPOS, the BTB wrapper (1 BTB mints 1,000,000 OPOS, redeemable any time), trades in dozens of Uniswap pairs and pays a 1% tax on every transfer back to the treasury. No emissions, no inflation: the yield is fees.

Contract: verified ERC20 with ERC1363 and ERC20Permit extensions, no mint function, no owner. Security contact: hello@btb.finance
